## Resource doctrine

Every employee and JARVIS must have protected, separated inference capacity:

- Dedicated JARVIS reserve for orchestration, delegation, recovery and critical decisions.
- Separate employee inference pool with priority-based quotas.
- Provider, model, quota, latency and cost monitoring.
- Preferred model to tested free fallback to local inference.
- No silent paid fallback.
- Checkpoint tasks and preserve state when providers fail.
- Emergency mode continues deterministic work and queues work that needs inference.
- Hard spending limits and explicit owner authorization for paid usage.
